Plan for 17 November to 15 December

One goal: turn this month's visibility into page-1 rankings, AI recommendations and meetings. Google and AI assistants already read and rank our content. What they still need is outside proof (reviews, directory listings, mentions and links), and consistent facts about the company.

Make every listing and page state the same facts (week 1)

Correct the old figures still live on 8 pages ("750 seats", "founded 2005") to the approved 1,000+ seats and 2016 launch, and fix the /call-center/ redirect. Then claim the RevenueBase listing and align Clutch, Outsource Accelerator and DesignRush.

Why: AI assistants repeat whatever the most sources agree on. Google AI Mode used RevenueBase for the exact "top BPOs in South Africa" question this month.

Win the first real client reviews

Send the agreed review request to 3 to 5 happy clients, first to GoodFirms (currently 0 reviews), with an anonymous option. Add the case studies to the GoodFirms portfolio.

Why: Gemini already quotes our GoodFirms profile. Reviews move us up that list, and Clutch already sends us enquiries.

Build authority for the 6 main service pages

Post the BPESA award to Outsource Accelerator, publish the November OA article ("5 reasons UK contact center operations are relocating to South Africa"), answer 5 journalist requests a week with Afrishore data, and pitch partner pages to non-competing CX and IT vendors. Every piece links to a service page, not the homepage.

Why: the content is ranking on pages 2 and 3. Outside mentions and links are what lift pages onto page 1.

Put the case studies to work, and add a fourth

Add case studies to the main navigation and the matching service pages, and use them in sales follow-ups. Draft a fourth, anonymised ITO case study on the multi-site home care client, which Jarrod has approved for anonymous reference.

Track which leads come from AI

Add "How did you hear about us?" (with ChatGPT and other AI options) to the contact form, and connect the form to Bitrix so leads arrive with their source. From the December report, meetings by division and AI-sourced leads come straight from the CRM.

Keep the content and outreach running

5 more articles are written and ready for upload (SA vs Philippines, travel call centers, outsourcing customer service to South Africa, tax preparation for CPA firms, global BPO), prioritising pages with real cost and comparison data, which AI cites most. Quant: read the message test results and keep the focus on insurers rather than consultancies.
