Where we are not there yet

Buyer clicks held steady (75 to 76). Almost all of them are branded searches or homepage visits, because our main service pages still sit on Google pages 2 and 3, where few people click. How we get there

Five steps for 17 November to 15 December, in priority order. Each one names the questions it moves.

08

The plan, 17 November to 15 December

One goal: turn this month's visibility into page-1 rankings, AI recommendations and meetings. Google and AI assistants already read and rank our content. What they still need is outside proof (directory listings, mentions and links), and consistent facts about the company.

Get Afrishore into the round-ups AI already cites (weeks 1 to 4)

  • Travel: pitch inclusion to the lists ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode cite (simplycontact.com, worldwidecallcenters.com, Global BPO Index), leading with the ferry case study (70 to 250 agents, 6 languages, 85% CSAT).
  • UK customer service: Outsource Accelerator and The Manifest round-ups.
  • AML: Horses for Sources and the compliance round-ups cited alongside Helpware and Kroll.
Why: on these three questions AI builds its answer from third-party lists, not company websites. Being on the list is the fastest route into the answer.
Moves:TravelUK customer serviceAML

Turn page-1 rankings into clicks (one session, week 1)

  • "Offshore administrative solutions": Google position 3 with 322 buyer impressions in 90 days, almost no clicks.
  • "BPO in anti money laundering": position 5 with 277 buyer impressions.
  • Rewrite each title and description for the buyer: keep the ranking phrase in the title, put cost, ramp time (4 to 6 weeks) and US/UK hours in the description. Change one thing at a time and log the date.
Why: these pages already rank. A better search snippet is the quickest way to more buyer visits.
Moves:AML

Put client proof on the pages that need to rank (weeks 1 to 2)

  • Add the ferry case study numbers to the travel page (page 3 today) and the travel article in this week's batch.
  • Neurology lab results onto the healthcare pages, the ARM results onto the collections page; case studies into the main navigation.
  • Draft a fourth, anonymised ITO case study (the multi-site home care client Jarrod approved).
Why: AI cites Afrishore wherever we publish real numbers. These pages have the traffic but not yet the proof.
Moves:TravelBest BPOs in SA

Build outside authority for the 6 main service pages (ongoing)

  • Publish the November Outsource Accelerator article ("5 reasons UK contact center operations are relocating to South Africa") and line up December's.
  • Agree 2 partner pages with non-competing vendors that sell to the same buyers (contact-center software, travel booking tech, collections software), each linking to the matching service page.
  • Turn the case studies and our cost, ramp-time and attrition data into one short, sourced data story a month that trade titles and AI assistants can quote.
  • Draft a journalist pitch plan for January: the 15 to 20 trade titles that cover CX outsourcing, travel and insurance operations, and the data story each one would take.
Why: the pages sit on Google pages 2 and 3. Outside mentions and links are what move them to page 1, and mentions are what AI repeats.
Moves:All five

Keep publishing 5 articles a week, more specialised

  • 5 articles written and ready now: travel call centers, outsourcing customer service to South Africa, SA vs Philippines, tax preparation for CPA firms, global BPO.
  • Go deeper on travel, our biggest buyer search cluster: the problems travel companies actually face, such as flight disruption and cancellation surges, peak-season staffing, refund and rebooking backlogs, and multilingual support.
  • Start integrating Afrishore Quant content into the weekly batches (actuarial offshoring for UK insurers).
